Join us for Photonics 2026 in Hyderabad

The 17th edition of India’s flagship global forum for optics, lasers, and photonic technologies, will be hosted by IIT Hyderabad, a premier hub of innovation and research. The event brings together global experts, industry leaders, and young researchers to share breakthroughs, foster collaborations, and shape the future of photonics.

Special Events

Industry–Academia Connect

PhotonBridge

A dynamic platform where researchers, industry leaders, and technologists converge to explore collaboration opportunities, discuss emerging trends, and accelerate the translation of photonics research into impactful real-world applications.

Women in Optics Forum

SheShines

A celebratory and empowering session highlighting the achievements, leadership, and contributions of women in optics and photonics. Engage in inspiring talks, mentorship discussions, and community building to support diversity in the field.

Startup Panel & Innovation Dialogue

LaunchLight

An energetic panel featuring founders, investors, and innovators driving photonics entrepreneurship. Learn about startup journeys, funding pathways, market needs, and how disruptive ideas in light-based technologies are shaping future industries.

PhD Clinic & Research Lounge

InsightLab

A dedicated space for doctoral students to present challenges, refine research ideas, seek expert feedback, and engage in one-on-one mentoring with senior researchers—designed to spark clarity, collaboration, and accelerated progress.
