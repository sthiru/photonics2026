Welcome to Hyderabad!!!

Hyderabad, the capital of Telangana, is a vibrant metropolis where rich history beautifully intersects with modern innovation. Known as the “City of Nawabs,” “Biryani City,” and the historic “City of Pearls,” it remains one of India’s most captivating destinations. Its architectural landmarks, bustling bazaars, and diverse cultural heritage create an atmosphere that is both timeless and dynamic. Adding to the city’s charm are the people of Hyderabad—kind, warm, and welcoming—whose gracious nature makes every visitor feel instantly at home.

Top Tourist Attractions in Hyderabad

A 16th-century architectural icon, surrounded by the vibrant Laad Bazaar, known for pearls and bangles.
A beautifully restored royal complex that highlights the architectural elegance and heritage of the Asaf Jahi dynasty.
A grand hilltop fortress offering panoramic city views and a captivating sound-and-light show showcasing its rich history.
A tranquil lake famous for its striking Buddha statue, perfect for boat rides and evening walks.
One of the world’s largest film studios, featuring extensive movie sets, guided tours, and exciting themed attractions.
A magnificent white-marble temple offering stunning views of the city skyline.
A vast museum housing rare manuscripts, sculptures, artefacts, and art collections from across the world.
A sprawling wildlife park featuring safari rides, a diverse range of animals, and a dedicated nocturnal house.
A lush forest reserve near Chilkur, home to deer, peacocks, and a variety of bird species.
A monumental statue of the 11th-century philosopher Sri Ramanuja, recognized as the world’s second-tallest sitting statue, located in Muchintal, Rangareddy district.
