About The Conference

The 17th edition of the International Conference on Fiber Optics and Photonics (PHOTONICS 2026), India’s flagship global forum for optics, lasers, and photonic technologies, will be hosted for the first time by IIT Hyderabad, a premier hub of innovation and research. Scheduled for December 2026, the event will open with a dedicated tutorial session and will bring together global experts, industry leaders, and young researchers to share breakthroughs, foster collaborations, and shape the future of photonics.

  • Sponsors 25
  • Sessions 40
  • Speakers 120 Professionals
  • Seats 800 People
